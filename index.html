About the archive

The Deep Cut is a small window into the Internet Archive's music collection. Each search brings five albums at random, each in a different style, leaving room for unexpected discoveries beyond recommendation feeds.

Albums come from across the archive's music collections: Jamendo, Netlabels, the Free Music Archive, Unlocked Recordings, digitized LPs, open source releases, hip-hop mixtapes, and Brazilian, Latin American and Colombian campesino music. Classical recordings are left out.

Every pick is random. Each search samples a different slice of the archive and shuffles it, so nothing is ranked by plays or by what you listened to before. The one exception is hip-hop mixtapes, which need at least 1,000 downloads to keep out anonymous DJ compilations.

Each album is checked before it reaches you: at least four tracks, an original cover, and no podcasts, radio or lectures. Releases from 2025 on also leave out albums that say they were made with AI tools such as Suno or Udio. Artist, year and region appear only when the record states them.

Your saved albums, liked tracks and listening history are kept in this browser, with no account. Visits are counted with Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies.

Get the app

Install The Deep Cut on your phone or computer. It opens full screen from its own icon and shows the track and cover on your lock screen and media keys. Free, no store, no account. Updates arrive on their own.

iPhone and iPad This device

  1. Open thedeepcut.vercel.app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⬆ (bottom of the screen on iPhone, top on iPad).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Apple does not let websites install themselves, so these taps are needed once.

Android This device

  1. Open the site in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app above, or menu ⋮ → Install app.

Samsung Internet and Edge work too: look for “Install” or “Add to home screen”.

Computer This device

  1. Windows, Mac or Linux: open the site in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click Install app above, or the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  3. Mac with Safari: File → Add to Dock.

Firefox cannot install sites as apps: use Chrome, Edge or Safari for this step.

TV This device

  1. Chromecast or Google TV: on Android, tap Play on TV here or the TV button in the player.
  2. Apple TV or AirPlay speaker: on iPhone or Mac, tap the TV button in the player, or pick AirPlay in Control Center.
  3. From a computer: Chrome menu ⋮ → Cast… → choose your TV. This mirrors the whole page.
  4. Smart TV with a browser (Samsung, LG, Android TV, Fire TV): open thedeepcut.vercel.app on the TV itself and browse with the remote: arrows move, OK selects, Back returns, play/pause and skip keys control the music.

Sending from a phone plays only the music on the TV; the page stays on the phone, which works as the remote.
